GIVEWELL BOARD MEETING #25 - DECEMBER 16, 2013

BOARD COMPOSITION

The board elected Rob Reich to the Clear Fund Board of Directors, expanding the board's composition. Rob Reich brought valuable expertise and perspective to the organization. All other directors continued in their existing roles: Tom Rutledge (President), Tim Ogden (Vice-President), Cari Tuna (Treasurer), Holden Karnofsky (Secretary), plus Elie Hassenfeld, Greg Jensen, Phil Steinmeyer, and Brigid Slipka who had joined earlier in 2013.

ORGANIZATIONAL CONTEXT

By late 2013, GiveWell had established itself as a significant force in evidence-based philanthropy. The organization had moved over $9 million to recommended charities in 2012, built a staff of 11 full-time employees (up from 5 at the start of the year), and developed a systematic research process. The partnership with Good Ventures had become central to operations, with shared office space and collaborative grantmaking.

However, challenges remained. Staff capacity continued to lag behind the work the organization wanted to accomplish. The co-founders remained heavily involved in core research work rather than transitioning to more strategic and oversight roles. GiveWell Labs had not yet produced concrete recommendations despite being identified as a priority. The organization faced a substantial projected operating deficit that required successful fundraising from a donor base primarily interested in giving to recommended charities rather than supporting GiveWell's operations.

The 2014 planning discussion would need to balance ambition with realism, identifying priorities that could be accomplished with available and projected resources while building toward longer-term goals. The conversation likely addressed questions of sequencing: what needed to happen first to enable subsequent progress, and what could be deferred without undermining core mission and strategy.
