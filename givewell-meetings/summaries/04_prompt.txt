5. 2008 budget.  Allow 30 minutes.

Staff will re-enter the meeting for a discussion of and vote on the budget
for May through December 2008.  See Attachment B.

6. Metrics.  Allow 30 minutes.

Board and Staff will discuss appropriate metrics for the coming year.
Staff moves to set a goal of $500,000 of “money moved” from new donors,
where “money moved” refers to the sum of (a) grants given directly by the
Clear Fund and (b) individual donations that are made to recommended
charities, primarily on our recommendation (including GiveWell Pledges).
See our plan for the coming year (included in Attachment A) for more on why
we feel this is the most important and appropriate metric for the coming
year.  We are also considering, but leaning against, hiring a third-party
evaluator to review our research.
