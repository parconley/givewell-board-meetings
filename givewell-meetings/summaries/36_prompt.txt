Exhibit 2  The following proposed change the by-laws clarifies a previously-approved change.  REVISION OF BY-LAWS REGARDING  THE AUTHORITY TO SIGN CHECKS AND NOTES   WHEREAS, The Clear Fund d/b/a GiveWell (“GiveWell”) is a New York not-for-profit corporation that conducts the majority of its operations in the State of California and is exempt from federal income tax under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code;   Whereas, the board wishes to amend the Adopted By-Laws of GiveWell (the “By-Laws”) to clarify that the Executive Director may delegate authority to sign checks and notes to employees of GiveWell.    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that Section 2 of Article 6 of the By-laws shall be deleted in its entirety, and the following new Section 2 of Article 6 of the By-laws shall be inserted in lieu thereof:    Section 2. Checks and Notes   Except as otherwise specifically determined by resolution of the board of directors, or as otherwise required by law, the executive director shall be authorized to sign checks, drafts, promissory notes, orders for the payment of money, and other evidence of indebtedness of the corporation that represent unbudgeted expenses in amounts of up to 10% of the previously board-approved 12 month expenses for GiveWell. All expenses exceeding such amounts shall be approved by the audit committee of the board.   The Executive Director may delegate authority to sign checks and notes to any employee of GiveWell. The Executive Director remains responsible for ensuring that all such checks and notes follow the applicable requirements laid out elsewhere in these By-Laws.    FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board hereby authorizes the officers and employees of GiveWell to restate the By-laws, as necessary, to reflect the foregoing amendments as well as any prior amendments to the By-Laws, and to make any necessary conforming changes related thereto.   FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board hereby authorizes the officers and employees of GiveWell to do and perform such further acts as may be necessary or advisable in order to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

Exhibit 3  The following proposed change the by-laws delegates authority to make grants from restricted funds to a subcommittee, to replace a quarterly full-board vote.   CREATION OF THE GRANT-MAKING SUBCOMMITTEE   WHEREAS, Article 5, Section 2 of the By-laws of The Clear Fund d/b/a GiveWell (“GiveWell”) provides that the Board of Directors (“Board”) of GiveWell may appoint such other committees as may from time to time be designated by resolution of the Board.   NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board hereby affirms and ratifies the formation of the Grant-Making Subcommittee to authorize the Executive Director to make charitable grants with funds restricted to supporting specific organizations or restricted to making grants at GiveWell's discretion;   FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Executive Director may delegate authority to request a vote from the Grant-Making Subcommittee to make charitable grants to any employee of GiveWell;  FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board hereby affirms and ratifies the appointment of the following members of the Board as members of the Grant-Making Subcommittee for a term that coincides with their term on the Board: Holden Karnofsky, Tom Rutledge, and Julia Wise.  FURTHER RESOLVED, that Holden Karnofsky shall serve as the Chair of the Grant-Making Subcommittee. FURTHER RESOLVED, that the officers of GiveWell are authorized to do and perform all such further acts and to execute such documents as shall be necessary or advisable in order to carry out the foregoing resolutions.
