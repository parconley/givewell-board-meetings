The brief discussion time (approximately 3 minutes) before the vote suggests the board had been well-prepared for this decision, with Steinmeyer likely having been vetted and discussed in advance of the formal meeting.

CONTEXT FROM RECENT STRATEGIC DECISIONS
The 2013 planning discussion likely addressed implementation of decisions made in mid-2012:

Research evolution: How to operationalize the shift toward more "foundation-like" research while maintaining transparency and commitment to serving retail donors. This would include balancing "passive" funding (no-strings-attached donations) with more "active" funding (designed strategies with restricted donations), and balancing "proven cost-effective" interventions with opportunities having less empirical evidence.

GiveWell Labs development: How to advance GiveWell Labs as a major research arm open to any giving opportunity regardless of form or sector, serving major donors while maintaining traditional charity evaluation work.

Good Ventures partnership: Implementing the partnership framework including office co-location (following or preparing for the San Francisco move), general support arrangements (up to 20 person-hours weekly with tracking), and maintaining clear boundaries to preserve independence.

Retail versus major funder balance: Operationalizing the commitment to provide enough outstanding giving opportunities for retail donors to absorb projected money moved while also investigating opportunities better suited for major funders.

- Potentially integrating new hires while some staff were adapting to San Francisco relocation
- Balancing multiple research tracks (traditional top charity identification, GiveWell Labs, regular updates on existing recommendations)

The organization was implementing an ambitious vision: evolving research methods and partnerships to find best possible giving opportunities while preserving credibility through transparency, sector-agnostic impact focus, and self-criticism. The extensive time devoted to open-ended planning discussion reflected both the complexity of this evolution and the board's commitment to thoughtful strategy rather than reactive growth.

By early 2013, GiveWell was positioned to operate at multiple scales simultaneously - serving retail donors with proven effective charities while exploring cutting-edge opportunities with major funders through GiveWell Labs, all while maintaining transparency standards that differentiated it from traditional foundations.
